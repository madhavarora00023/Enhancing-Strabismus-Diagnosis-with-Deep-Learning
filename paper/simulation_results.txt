Simulation Results

In this study,the classification of strabismus was focused
using deep learning. The proposed models successfully per-
formed the multiclass classification using different Neural-Network Models achieving high accuracy as shown in Table.I in detecting and classifying different types of strabismus which are represented
in confusion matrices as shown in Fig. 1, and 2.
These confusion matrices typically give us a close analysis
of models' performance and key performance metrics such
as accuracy, precision,recall, and F1-score were computed.The
simulation results define the efficacy of using deep learning
models for strabismus classification, demonstrating promising
clinical applicability.
Further, the average angle of deviation varies across the
five classes, with values, progressively increasing from nor-
mal (5.05) to exotropia (8.49), esotropia (10.64), hypotropia
(14.29), and hypertropia (20.52). This trend suggests that
angular deviation serves as a reliable parameter for assessing
strabismus severity, with higher values corresponding to more
pronounced ocular misalignment.
